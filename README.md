# Privacy-policy-for-SpaceMeter
Privacy Policy
Effective Date: Nov 25
App Name: SpaceMeter

Overview
SpaceMeter ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how SpaceMeter handles your information when you use our mobile application.
Data Collection & Usage
•
Zero Data Collection: SpaceMeter is a 100% offline personal storage planner. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, device files, or usage analytics.
•
Local Storage Only: All storage records, custom categories, scenarios, and user settings you enter are stored exclusively on your local device using Android Jetpack DataStore Preferences. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties.
•
No Third-Party Services: We do not integrate any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, advertising networks, or backend servers.
Permissions
SpaceMeter requires no special permissions to function. It operates without accessing the internet, camera, microphone, contacts, or internal device storage files.
Children's Privacy
SpaceMeter does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected from any user, the app is completely safe for users of all ages.
Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within this document with an updated effective date.
